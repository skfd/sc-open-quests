Open Quests

A home-screen widget: how many StreetComplete quests are open in a city, or for one quest type in it, and how that has moved. The numbers are this site's, a day or more old, and they belong to the place, not to you.

Android

In progress

Home-screen widget, from Google Play

A debug build runs on the emulator. The Play release waits on a developer account and its 14-day closed test with 12 testers.

iPhone, Scriptable

In progress

A script for the free Scriptable app, no App Store release

Runs on the owner's iPhone. Its numbers are tested against the same files as Android's.

Download open-quests.js

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store (free).
  2. Tap the button above, then in Files move open-quests.js from Downloads to iCloud Drive › Scriptable (open Scriptable once first, so the folder exists).
  3. Add a Scriptable widget to the home screen: small, medium or large.
  4. Long-press it › Edit Widget, choose the script, and type a city as the Parameter: Berlin, or Berlin; bus stop shelter for one quest type. Empty shows Toronto.
