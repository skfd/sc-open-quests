Privacy

This site and the Open Quests widgets have no accounts, no ads and no cookies. This page says what is stored, and who sees a request.

Who runs this

StreetComplete statistics (street-complete-statistics.comentality.com) and the Open Quests widgets are a personal project run by skfd, in Canada. It is independent: not part of, and not endorsed by, StreetComplete or OpenStreetMap.

What the site knows about you

Nothing that names you. There is nothing to sign in to and no form to fill. The numbers on the site are counts of map data per place; they say nothing about any mapper, and the site does not read anyone's edits to show them.

What is kept in your browser

  • No cookies. The site sets none, and neither do the services below on its behalf.
  • One list of recent cities. The page remembers the last few cities you opened, so it can offer them again. That list lives in your browser's local storage under the key sc-open-quests-cities. It is never sent anywhere, and clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

Who sees a request

Loading a page means your browser talks to these services. Each one receives your IP address and browser details, as any web server does.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its data files (sc-data.comentality.com). It keeps its own server logs and gives the site totals such as requests per day. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • GoatCounter counts page views. It receives the page's address, the page that linked here, and your browser, system, language, screen width and country. By its own privacy policy it stores no IP address and nothing in your browser; it holds your IP and browser details in memory for up to eight hours to tell repeat views apart, and keeps the rest as totals. It is run from Ireland on servers in Finland and Germany. A content blocker that stops gc.zgo.at does not break the site.
  • OpenStreetMap's tile servers draw the background of the Map tab, and only when you open it. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.
  • GitHub holds the source code and the place requests. Following a link there, or asking for a place by opening an issue, is under GitHub's privacy statement; an issue is public and carries your GitHub name.

The site loads no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere else, shows no ads, and sells or shares nothing, because it holds nothing to share.

The Open Quests widgets

The Android app and the iPhone script fetch the same public data files as this site, from street-complete-statistics.comentality.com and sc-data.comentality.com, whenever they refresh. Those requests reach Cloudflare like any other. The widgets have no account, no analytics and no advertising identifier, do not use your location, and send nothing about you. The city you chose stays on your phone, apart from being part of the address of the file the widget asks for.

Questions and requests

The site keeps no record of you, so there is nothing of yours for it to show, correct or delete. For anything else, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public: do not put anything private in one.

Changes

If the site starts to store or send anything more, this page changes first. Its history is public in the source repository.
